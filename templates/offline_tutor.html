COMMISSION NOTICE

10% platform commission

LearnPath charges a 10% commission on every booking, taken from the payment the student makes to the tutor. Tutors must acknowledge this before they can receive bookings.

  • The commission applies to every booking on LearnPath. There are no free demo classes.
  • The commission is deducted from the student's payment to the tutor. The tutor receives the remaining 90%.
  • If a booking is refunded, the 10% commission is not returned.
  • Example: on a session of ₹500, ₹50 goes to LearnPath and the tutor receives ₹450.
  • A tutor who has not acknowledged these terms cannot be booked.
